Why this question matters

Many retirement discussions focus on how much money you need. But an equally important question is how long your savings will last once spending, inflation and investment returns start interacting over time.

What feels comfortable at first can look very different after 15 or 20 years if your spending keeps rising while your savings begin to shrink.

What this calculator shows

This tool estimates how long your money may last by applying annual investment growth, subtracting annual spending, and then increasing that spending by inflation each year.

It is a simplified model, but it helps illustrate a powerful point: small differences in spending, returns or inflation can produce very different outcomes over time.

A better question is often not “How much money do I have?” but “How long will it last?”
