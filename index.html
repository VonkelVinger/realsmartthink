Why RealSmartThink exists

Many things that seem obvious turn out to depend on the assumptions behind them. Averages can mislead. Risks can be misunderstood. Headlines can flatten important differences.

RealSmartThink exists to make those questions clearer through simple tools, better framing and more careful reasoning.
